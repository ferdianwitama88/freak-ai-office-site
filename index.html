AI-ASSISTED CONTENT OPERATIONS

Content planning, organized.

Freak AI Office is a private creator dashboard that helps its owner organize short-form video ideas, prepare draft scripts, track production tasks, and review content before publishing.

The website is an information page, not a public login portal. Production, review, and account connections run in a separate, privately controlled application.

Plan

Organize content topics and maintain a queue of drafts.

Create

Prepare AI-assisted scripts and production notes with human verification.

Review

Keep content under owner review before publication, including source and media-rights checks.

TikTok integration status

OAuth-based account connection and TikTok posting features are under development and subject to TikTok approval. This informational website does not log in to TikTok, collect TikTok credentials, or publish content.
